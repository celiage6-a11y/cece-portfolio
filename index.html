More about Cece.

I'm a second-year Digital Design student at Ulster University, with a particular love for UX research and visual design.

I have always been fascinated by how differently we as humans can think, communicate, and experience life. How is it possible that two people can have completely different experiences of the same thing? UX allows me to channel this curiosity.

I'm naturally curious, research-driven and always willing to question my first solution, because I believe good design starts with understanding people, not just pixels.
